Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

This page (the mcbflow.com hub) and its Account page are a free personal project run by Matthew Broyer. You don’t need an account to use anything here. The site does not load advertising, analytics, fonts, or scripts from other companies’ servers, and its code does not set cookies. The account sign-in library (supabase-js) is open-source code hosted on this site.

What this hub stores on your device

  • Theme preference (light/dark)
  • Pinned app IDs
  • Local launch counts for listed apps
  • Whether you dismissed the local-storage notice

These values are kept in your browser’s local storage for this site. This page does not send them anywhere. They stay until you delete them with Delete Local Data in the footer or clear your browser’s site data. Export Hub Data saves a copy to your own device.

What leaves your browser

  • This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as it does for every GitHub Pages site. Those logs are controlled by GitHub, not by this page’s code, and are covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • If you press Check status, your browser sends one request to each listed app’s address to see whether it responds. Those sites’ hosts can see that request the way they would see a visit. Nothing from this page’s local storage is included.
  • If you open a linked app, your browser goes to that site.
  • If you click “Support this site,” your browser opens buymeacoffee.com, a separate service with its own privacy policy. This page does not send it any of your local storage data.
  • If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you write, and I use it to reply to you.

Optional account

If you create an account, this is what’s stored and why:

  • Your email address and password, to sign you in. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash by the sign-in service; nobody can read them.
  • A display name, if you enter one, and the date you signed up.
  • Synced app data: the text records, settings and progress of apps you sign in to, so they match across your devices. Files such as photos, PDFs, audiobooks and attachments are never uploaded and stay on each device.
  • Your browser keeps a sign-in token in local storage for each site where you’re signed in (not a cookie). Signing out removes it.

Two services handle this on my behalf. Supabase hosts the account database and sign-in (servers in the eastern United States; Supabase privacy policy). Resend sends account emails such as sign-up confirmations and password resets from no-reply@mail.mcbflow.com (Resend privacy policy). Your data isn’t sold, shared for advertising, or used for anything other than running your account. Each account can read only its own data.

On the Account page you can export all your synced data as a file, delete one app’s synced data, change your email or password, or delete your account. Deleting your account permanently removes your login, profile and all synced data. Data saved inside each app on your own devices isn’t affected. Synced data is kept until you delete it or your account.

Linked apps

Listed tools (for example money.mcbflow.com) are separate websites, each on its own subdomain of mcbflow.com, with their own privacy information. This policy covers only this hub page.
